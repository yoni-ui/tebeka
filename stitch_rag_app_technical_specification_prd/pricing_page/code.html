Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our legal assistant AI.

How does the billing work?

We bill monthly or annually. Annual plans receive a 20% discount. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time through your account settings.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel at any time. You will continue to have access to your plan's features until the end of your current billing period.

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. We do not use your private legal documents to train our global models. Your data is isolated in secure, encrypted storage environments.
