Master Service Agreement - TechFlow Inc.

Draft v2.4

MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of October 24, 2023, by and between TechFlow Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Client") and LexiGen Solutions ("Provider").

1. Scope of Services

Provider shall perform the services described in each Statement of Work ("SOW") executed by the parties. Each SOW shall be subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

2. Limitation of Liability HIGH RISK

Except for gross negligence or willful misconduct, in no event shall Provider's total aggregate liability for any claims, losses, or damages arising out of or in connection with this agreement exceed five times (5x) the total fees paid by Client to Provider during the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to such liability.

3. Intellectual Property

All deliverables provided to Client under an SOW shall be the sole and exclusive property of Client upon full payment of fees. Provider retains ownership of its pre-existing materials used in the performance of the services.

4. Termination STANDARD

Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience upon thirty (30) days written notice to the other party. In the event of termination, Client shall pay Provider for all services performed through the effective date of termination.

Think of this contract like a high-stakes group project. The "Limitation of Liability" part is basically saying if they mess up, they'll only pay you back up to five times what you already paid them—which sounds like a lot, but if your business loses millions because of their error, it's actually a huge trap. The termination clause is fair: either side can quit with a month's notice.
