I'm reviewing a residential lease in California. What are the legal requirements for a security deposit, and can my landlord charge a non-refundable cleaning fee?

Under California Civil Code Section 1950.5, there are specific protections for residential tenants regarding security deposits.

Maximum Limits

Landlords cannot charge more than 2 months' rent for unfurnished units, or 3 months' rent for furnished units.

Cleaning Fees

In California, all security deposits are refundable. No portion of the deposit can be designated as "non-refundable," including cleaning fees.

menu_book Key Provisions

  • The deposit must be returned within 21 days of the tenant vacating the premises.
  • Landlords must provide an itemized statement for any deductions over $125.
